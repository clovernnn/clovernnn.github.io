Recruiter questions that matter

  • For this exact role, legal entity and work location, when can PERM start?
  • Is PERM batch or individual?
  • Are recent candidates in this job family/location passing the labor-market test?
  • Are layoffs, cooldown periods, recruitment pauses or government actions affecting new cases?
  • Will the company support an H-1B transfer immediately, and which costs/fees are company-paid?

Status methodology

GREEN Recent evidence supports favorable PERM initiation/movement and no known major blocker.

YELLOW Sponsorship exists, but timing, LMT, layoffs, enforcement or evidence quality creates material uncertainty.

RED A known suspension, debarment or comparable barrier currently makes PERM unreliable.

Official government sources override community reports. Community posts are used only for employer policies that are rarely published, such as Day-1 timing, batch cycles and LMT experience.
